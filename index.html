I'm Queralt Martín-Saladich, PhD

Welcome to my portfolio.

I hold a PhD in Computational Biomedical Engineering with a background in Fundamental Physics and 6+ years of experience applying AI and machine learning to healthcare challenges. My work focuses on medical image processing, computational modeling, and data-driven approaches to advance biomedical research and clinical applications. By integrating programming, mathematics, and medical knowledge, I develop tools that support clinical decision-making and enable personalized patient management.

Happy to connect!📩

PhD thesis defense

I am immensely grateful to both of my supervisors, Miguel Angel Gonzalez Ballester at Universitat Pompeu Fabra and J.
